Tofu Pasta Sauce Recipe


Description

A high-protein tofu pasta sauce that is quick and easy to make, while also being fully vegan-friendly! Super tasty!

PLEASE NOTE: The origin of the recipe is not mine. Here is the link to the original recipe!

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces dry pasta

Sauce:

  • 14 oz firm tofu
  • 1/4 cup + 2 tbsp extra/virgin olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 cup nutritional yeast
  • 2 tbsp mellow miso
  • 6 sun-dried tomatoes
  • Fresh lemon juice
  • Crushed red pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta according to packet directions until al dente. Reserve two cups of the pasta cooking water.
  2. Place the tofu, olive oil or vegetable broth, garlic, nutritional yeast, and miso in a food processor. Process until smooth, scraping the sides of the container with a spatula as needed.
  3. Transfer the sauce to a large saute pan. Add one cup of the reserved pasta cooking water and whisk to combine. Cook over low heat, stirring frequently. When the sauce is warm, mix in the pasta, and sun-dried tomatoes. Cook over low heat until warmed through, adding more pasta cooking water as needed.
  4. Add fresh lemon juice, and crushed red pepper.
